# JANBHASHA Open Dataset & Model Licensing Compliance

All datasets and models integrated into JANBHASHA adhere strictly to their respective open-access, research, and non-commercial educational licenses.

---

## 1. Karya Hindi -> Mundari Translation Dataset
- **Official Repository**: [https://github.com/karya-inc/dataset-hindi-mundari-translation](https://github.com/karya-inc/dataset-hindi-mundari-translation)
- **License**: CC-BY-4.0 / Open Data for AI Development
- **Allowed Use**: Research, training, educational applications, non-commercial offline inference.
- **Attribution**: Karya Inc., "Hindi-Mundari Parallel Translation Corpus", 2024.
- **Dataset Size**: 17,826 verified parallel pairs.

---

## 2. AI4Bharat BPCC (Bharat Parallel Corpus Collection) - Santali
- **Official Source**: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/BPCC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/BPCC)
- **Language Code**: `sat_Olck` (Ol Chiki Script)
- **License**: CC-BY-NC-4.0
- **Allowed Use**: Research, non-commercial educational translation.
- **Attribution**: AI4Bharat, IIT Madras, "IndicTrans2: Towards High-Quality Machine Translation for Indic Languages", 2023.

---

## 3. Microsoft MunTTS (Mundari Text-to-Speech)
- **Official Repository**: [https://github.com/microsoft/MunTTS-A-Text-to-Speech-System-For-Mundari](https://github.com/microsoft/MunTTS-A-Text-to-Speech-System-For-Mundari)
- **License**: MIT License / MSR Research
- **Allowed Use**: Academic & educational deployment of VITS acoustic models.

---

## 4. Meta MMS (Massively Multilingual Speech) -- Santali & Mundari
- **Official Model Cards**:
  - Santali ASR/TTS: `facebook/mms-1b-all` / `facebook/mms-tts-sat`
  - Mundari ASR/TTS: `facebook/mms-1b-all` / `facebook/mms-tts-unr`
- **License**: CC-BY-NC 4.0
- **Allowed Use**: Offline speech synthesis and speech recognition for low-resource tribal languages.

---

## 5. CIIL Bhasha Sanchika -- Central Institute of Indian Languages
- **Official Repository**: [https://sanchika.ciil.org/](https://sanchika.ciil.org/)
- **License**: Government of India Open Access for Vernacular Pedagogy.
- **Allowed Use**: Tribal dialect research, vocabulary preservation, and primary school curriculum alignment.
